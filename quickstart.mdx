The Volimox team runs your setup with you. You share your business details, service area, pricing, and the tools you use. Once the required connections are ready, your account is created.

## Setup steps

1. **Discovery:** We confirm your brand, service area, call volume, and current system together.
2. **Setup:** The Volimox team prepares a configuration specific to your business.
3. **Brand and content:** Your logo, images, company details, and basic text are added. The Volimox team can do this step for you.
4. **Account:** The Volimox team creates your business account. You sign in at your own login address with your own password.
5. **Acceptance:** Your data, prices, payments, assignments, notifications, and call handoff are checked together.

<Note>
  If a connection is not ready during setup, Volimox does not show it as active. Any missing connection is clearly listed during the acceptance step.
</Note>

## Your first sign-in

When we create your account, your password is shown once. Keep it in a safe place. If you forget your password, the Volimox team will reset it.

<Warning>
  Do not share your password by email, chat, or in documents.
</Warning>
