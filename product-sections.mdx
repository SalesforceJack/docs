title: "Product sections"
description: "The main sections of the Volimox panel and what each one is for"
---

Your daily work in the Volimox panel is organized into six main sections. This page explains what each one is used for.

| Section | What it is used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | A daily overview, upcoming tasks, pending work, and situations that need attention. |
| **Reservations** | New bookings, upcoming and past trips by date, search, and call and booking records. |
| **Operations** | Manual assignment, drivers, vehicle availability, partners, documents, and notification results. |
| **Customers** | Passengers and their history, potential customers, business accounts, and contacts. |
| **Payments** | Payment status linked to reservations, saved invoices, and the Volimox commission summary. |
| **Website** | Brand, images, basic text, theme, and the business settings you are allowed to change. |

<Note>
  All of these sections may not be available for every business. The sections you can use depend on your agreed setup scope.
</Note>
