Volimox turns phone booking requests into reliable quotes, reservations, and daily operations for transport businesses. Volimox Voice answers calls, and it works together with management screens that track reservations and operations.

## Who it is for

Volimox is built for businesses that want to move phone requests into a clear, organized process. There are two main ways to use it:

- **You already have a system:** Volimox Voice answers calls, completes supported actions, and hands off to your team when needed. You do not need to replace your current reservation system.
- **You do not have a system:** Your brand's website, online booking, price quotes, payment flow, customer records, manual dispatch, and Volimox Voice are set up together.

## How to get started

The Volimox team reviews your business with you, prepares the setup, and creates your account. For details, see [Getting started](/quickstart).
